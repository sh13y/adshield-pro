# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date**: May 2026

---

## 1. Introduction

AdShield Pro ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Extension operates, what data it collects (if any), and how it respects your fundamental right to privacy.

---

## 2. Data Collection & Storage

**What We Collect**
- AdShield Pro does **not collect** any personal data
- No browsing history is recorded
- No IP addresses are logged
- No analytics data is transmitted

**What We Store Locally**
The Extension stores only essential settings on your device:
- User preferences (enabled/disabled status)
- Block counter statistics
- Custom rule configurations (if applicable)

All data remains on your local machine. Nothing is transmitted to external servers.

---

## 3. How We Use Data

**Local Processing Only**
- Ad blocking rules are processed locally
- Statistics are calculated on your device
- Settings are stored in browser storage

**No External Communication**
- The Extension does not communicate with our servers
- No data is sent to third parties
- No cloud synchronization occurs

**Scope of Operation**
- The Extension operates exclusively on youtube.com
- It does not monitor or log activity on other websites
- It does not track your viewing habits

---

## 4. Permissions & Their Use

The Extension requests specific permissions, each limited to essential functions:

### 4.1 declarativeNetRequest
**Purpose**: Enables network-level ad blocking
- Intercepts HTTP requests to ad-serving domains
- Blocks requests before they load
- Operates according to predefined rules

### 4.2 storage
**Purpose**: Saves user preferences and settings
- Stores extension configuration locally
- Maintains block counter statistics
- No cloud backup or synchronization

### 4.3 tabs
**Purpose**: Identifies YouTube tabs
- Detects when you're on youtube.com
- Enables targeted protection on YouTube only
- Does not track tab content or history

### 4.4 activeTab
**Purpose**: Accesses current tab information
- Determines if current tab is a YouTube page
- No recording or transmission of tab contents

### 4.5 scripting
**Purpose**: Injects content modification scripts
- Removes ad elements from the page
- Blocks overlay advertisements
- Operates only on YouTube
